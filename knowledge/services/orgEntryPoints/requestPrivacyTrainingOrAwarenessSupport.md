# Request privacy training or awareness support

- Description: Delivers privacy training, awareness sessions, issue-driven briefings, and audience-specific learning support.
- When to use:
  - A team needs onboarding, refresher training, or role-based privacy guidance.
  - A new process, control, or incident theme needs targeted awareness support.
  - A business area wants reusable privacy learning content or a live training session.
- When not to use:
  - The need is a case review or project assessment rather than training.
  - Existing material is sufficient and no new support is needed.

## Inputs

- Requesting team, audience, and expected participation scope.
- Training objective and the behavior or decision change needed.
- Topic, scenario, control area, or incident theme to cover.
- Preferred delivery format, timing, duration, and language needs.
- Existing materials, policies, examples, or decks available for reuse.
- Evidence, completion tracking, or acknowledgement requirements.

## Outputs

- Training plan or briefing outline matched to the audience.
- Session materials, reusable learning content, or facilitator notes.
- Scheduled session, assignment setup, or publishing path for delivery.
- Attendance, completion, or acknowledgement records where needed.
- Follow-up actions, refresher recommendations, or content updates.
